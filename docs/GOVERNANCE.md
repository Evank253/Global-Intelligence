# KCN Intelligence OS v4 — Governance & AI Ethics Policy Specification

## Core Governance Directives

### 1. Zero Black-Box Guarantee
Every decision, plan, software synthesis artifact, and research output generated by KCN Intelligence OS produces an immutable **6-Question Accountability Passport**:
- **Risk Score:** `black_box_risk_score: 0.00`
- **Unalterable Audit Lineage:** Preserved in SHA-256 state ledger signatures (`sig_audit_*`).

### 2. Strict Expected Calibration Error Bounding ($ECE \le 0.035$)
- All agent confidence scores are continuously calibrated against empirical verification datasets.
- Automated **Humility Disclaimers** trigger whenever agent confidence exceeds domain evidence coverage.

### 3. Human Review Co-Signing Thresholds
Operations exceeding predefined impact thresholds trigger mandatory human escalation gates in the Executive Cortex:
- High-risk domain actions (e.g., real-world infrastructure control, clinical medical recommendations, legal binding contract execution) require co-signature by a human operator before staging.

### 4. Regulatory Framework Alignment
- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF):** Controls mapped across Govern, Map, Measure, and Manage functions.
- **EU AI Act Compliance:** High-risk AI system logging, transparency, human oversight, and data governance standards.
